About Me

I am an Information Technology educator and practitioner with experience spanning higher education, software development, database administration, government ICT, research, innovation, and student mentoring.

I am currently pursuing a Doctor in Information Technology at State University of Northern Negros, and I am employed as Assistant Professor II at Iloilo State University of Fisheries Science and Technology, College of Informatics and Computing Innovations. My work combines teaching, software engineering, database systems, artificial intelligence, digital transformation, cybersecurity awareness, and applied research.

I previously worked at DSWD Region VI under the Regional Information and Communications Technology Management Section, where I served as a Computer Programmer III and was promoted to Information Technology Officer I - Database Administrator. These roles shaped my understanding of how technology supports public service, data management, and organizational effectiveness.

In my academic role, I teach and mentor students across a range of technical subjects, including Object-Oriented Programming, Database Systems, Integrative Programming, Desktop Application Development, Information Security, and Software Development.
